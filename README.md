27 stories across regions and fields: the Gulf war widening (Iran strikes US bases in Bahrain), COP31 collapsing with no deal, the Monday market rebound after the AI rout, NATO emergency talks after Russian missiles enter Poland, Ukraine sinking a Russian warship, and more — drawn as a newspaper front page.

**v3:** back to the readable 27-story layout of v1 (1 hero + 3 medium + 16 grid + 7 ticker) after v2's denser 42-story layout ranked worse — round 1 (v1): 187 of 7,226, score 711,309; round 2 (v2): 2,676 of 9,643, score 573,279. Same layout as v1, but the stories are the strongest of the full day, not the morning-only pool.
